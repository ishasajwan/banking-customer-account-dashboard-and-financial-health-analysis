# banking-customer-account-dashboard-and-financial-health-analysis
This project has been developed as part of the AICW (Artificial Intelligence in the Contemporary World) Fellowship Program at Graphic Era University, aimed at enhancing practical skills in data analytics, business intelligence, and AI tools.

Title:
RetainX: Smart Customer Churn Analysis using Power BI & AI

Objectives: 
•  Data Acquisition: Audit the Project_Retention_Churn_Analysis.xlsx structure to map 14 customer variables across 10,000 records for churn modeling.
•  Preprocessing: Execute data cleaning to ensure 100% accuracy in date formats, medical codes, or customer identifiers like CustomerId while eliminating duplicates.
•  Domain Analysis: Conduct a deep-dive analysis into demographic churn drivers, specifically examining why churn in Germany is double that of France.
•  Visual Communication: Build an "Executive Dashboard" to present high-level KPIs like total customers, average credit score, and overall churn rate.
•  AI Integration: Use AI tools to automate the generation of data summaries and optimize prompting for reporting on customer behavior trends.
•  Targeted Strategy: Develop a predictive segmentation model to identify high-balance, low-activity customers who are most likely to exit.

Key  Findings: 
•  Regional Risk: Germany exhibits the highest churn rate at 32.4%, which is significantly higher than France and Spain, marking it as a critical area for intervention.
•  Efficiency Gain: Using AI tools like Microsoft Copilot reduced the time required for data cleaning and DAX formula creation by approximately 60% compared to manual Excel methods.
•  Demographic Vulnerability: Customers within the 40–60 age bracket represent the highest attrition group, indicating a need for targeted loyalty programs for middle-aged segments.
•  Engagement Correlation: Active membership is a primary deterrent for churn; inactive members are nearly twice as likely to exit regardless of their account balance.
•  Data Integrity: The cleaning process standardized 10,000 records across 14 variables, ensuring that dashboard visualizations like "Churn by Geography" are 100% accurate and free from naming inconsistencies.
